# Arena

Mode: Experience. The audience is a judge or desktop player opening the game for a short run. The premise, controls, and start action must fit into the first viewport. The brief pins the Halloween neon world and the code-drawn artwork. The presentation is code-led.

## Direction contract

**THESIS**
The title is the entrance to the same arena where the action happens. The first thing players learn is that walking over a costume replaces their power.

**OWN-WORLD**
Use deep violet space and a faint grid, sharp planed silhouettes, bone-white type, and the brief's orange, cyan, green and violet cues. Build the shapes, motion and light in Canvas. Keep the chrome thin and square.

**STORY**
See the wardrobe trade on the title screen, enter with one action, read threats around the edges, interrupt the fight for an upgrade, and see the silhouette and weapon change together after a swap.

**FIRST VIEWPORT**
A single bounded arena fills the desktop. An off-centre carving takes up the right of the title state, and the game title and entrance sit low and left in the negative space. Health and wave read from the upper left during play, with progress and costume at the lower edge. The signature interaction is the costume pickup, with a short ring at impact and new color, silhouette, and ability label on the same frame.

**FORM**
Code-led arcade title and combat field with the brief's pinned direction. The same arena surface hosts start, pause, upgrade, and results.

**FINISH**
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
